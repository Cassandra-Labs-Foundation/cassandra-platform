# Explorer comprehension questions

A reviewer should be able to answer each question from the explorer's pages alone. The weekly review gives a reader who has never seen the system only screenshots of the explorer and this file, then grades the answers. A question that keeps failing points at a view that does not say what it should.

Answer each question in one or two sentences, and name the screenshot you relied on.

1. A partner sends a $30,000 transfer from an account that has sent no other money today. What happens to it?
2. A $15,000 transfer is sent from an account well under its daily outflow limit. What happens, and is the transfer stopped?
3. What happens to a transfer of exactly $10,000 under the large-movement rule?
4. Who may decide whether a suspicious activity report is filed, and which decider is refused even with the right role?
5. When an alert is escalated to an investigation, how long does the investigation have, and when does that clock start?
6. Which database tables does `transfers.ts` reference?
7. Which gate checks does `transfers.ts` implement, and which of them blocks a movement?
8. How does the staff console read the core's data?
9. Where do account balances live as the source of truth, and how does the core learn that they changed?
10. Is control BSA-06 passing, and in which test tiers?
11. Is the explorer a compliance verdict? What does each of its two layers establish?
12. Starting from the overview, which boxes do you open, in order, to reach the rule that decides whether a movement is large?
